What a dripping faucet wastes and what causes it

If your faucet will not shut off or a supply line is spraying, call our 24/7 line now. While you wait, turn the small oval-handled stop valve under the sink clockwise until the water stops. If there is no stop valve, or it will not turn, close the main shutoff for the house and set a bucket under the leak.

Faucet repair means replacing the worn part inside a tap, usually a rubber washer, O-ring, seat or cartridge, or replacing the whole fixture when the body has corroded. A drip starts when that part no longer seals against the pressure behind it. Older two-handle taps wear out washers, and single-handle taps wear out cartridges.

The waste is not only water. Ocoee's residential water rate rises in steps as monthly use climbs, so the gallons lost at the top of your bill cost the most. A leak at the base of the spout also soaks the cabinet floor below, and a drip that is really a leak inside the wall needs leak detection, not a new washer.

Look for replacement faucets and cartridges marked as meeting NSF/ANSI 61, the national standard for materials that touch drinking water.

Faucet will not shut off or a line is spraying

Some faucet problems cannot wait for a regular business day. These four send water out faster than a bucket can catch it.

Handle turns but water keeps running

A stripped or broken cartridge no longer closes the port. Shut the stop valve under the sink, or the main shutoff for a tub or shower, and stop forcing the handle.

Supply line spraying under the sink

Braided hoses fail at the crimp or the nut. Close the stop valve, then check the cabinet floor, because wet particleboard swells and breaks down fast.

Water coming through the wall or ceiling below

A leak behind a tub or shower valve is inside the wall. A technician must open the wall to reach it, so cutting the water early limits the damage.

The stop valve will not turn

Old stops seize with mineral crust and can snap if forced. Do not lean on them with pliers. Use the main shutoff and tell dispatch the valve is stuck.

Signs a faucet needs repair before it fails

Most faucets warn you for weeks. Catching the warning turns a night call into a planned visit.

Drips after the handle is off

Water is slipping past a worn seal. It will not stop by itself, and it speeds up as the seat erodes.

Handle feels loose, stiff or gritty

A worn cartridge or a stripped handle screw. Gritty movement often means scale inside the cartridge.

Weak flow or a spray that goes sideways

Often a clogged aerator, the small screen at the spout tip. Unscrew it and rinse it. If one tap stays weak, the supply line or stop valve is next.

What a faucet visit covers, in order

We do not diagnose a faucet by phone. We ask about the brand, the handle type and where the water shows, so the technician knows what to look for.

Stop the water and look around

The technician closes the stop valve or main, then checks the stop valves, supply lines and drain connections before touching the faucet. A tired stop valve is often blamed on the fixture.

Pull the part and test the repair

Handle, cap and retaining nut come off, and the cartridge, washer or seat is checked for wear and scoring. After the repair, hot and cold are run, and the stop valves are checked for full shutoff.

Repair or replace

Repair when the body is sound and the part is available. Replace when the body is pitted or cracked, the seat is scored past resurfacing, the cartridge is discontinued, or repeat repairs cost close to a new faucet. A toilet that keeps running is a different fault, handled under toilet repair.

Ocoee water, purple lines and hose bibs

Four local facts change how faucet and spigot work is judged here.

Moderate water hardness

Ocoee Utilities' 2025 water quality report lists hardness at 5 to 6 grains per gallon, which the report's own scale calls moderate. That is enough for scale to clog aerators and cartridges over time, so rinse aerators now and then. Forest Lake Estates is served by Orange County, so check which utility bills you.

Purple reclaimed lines are not drinking water

The city says reclaimed water is not drinking water and that cross connections between reclaimed and potable plumbing are not allowed. Its reclaim pipe and meter boxes are marked purple. Never fill a drinking container from a spigot on a purple line, and if you cannot tell which line a spigot feeds, ask Utilities at 407-905-3159.

Hose bibs and backflow

Orange County Utilities' guide for its own customers names garden hose cases such as spraying lawn chemicals and filling a pool with the hose end underwater. In both, dirty water can flow backward into the house supply. A hose bib that leaks at the stem needs repair, not tape.

Where the city's pipe ends

The city's service request form puts the meter and the system up to it on the city, and everything from the meter to the house on the customer. A dripping tub valve or spigot is yours to fix. A leak at the meter box or the street side is not.
