Clogged again

Snaked last month and clogged again? That is the jetting question

A line that backs up weeks after a snaking is the usual reason Ocoee homeowners and property managers ask about hydro jetting. If water is rising now, our 24/7 line is open, and until the plumber arrives, stop sending water down that line.

A cable usually punches a hole through the blockage and leaves the coating on the pipe wall. Think of a straw pushed through thick milkshake. Water drains for a while, then the walls catch new debris and the hole closes.

Hydro jetting sends pressurized water through a hose and nozzle so the spray scours the inside of the pipe and carries the debris downstream. It cleans the pipe. It does not repair one, and that limit shapes everything below.

Cable machine or jetting: what each leaves behind

The two tools solve different problems. Picking the wrong one is how clogs come back.

After a cable

  • A clear channel through the center of the blockage.
  • Grease and scale still stuck to the walls.
  • Roots chopped back but their stubs still in the joint.
  • Fine for a fresh, single-point clog.

After jetting

  • Walls rinsed along the length the nozzle travels.
  • Grease and silt carried away instead of pushed aside.
  • Roots cut back, though a root-filled joint still needs a repair look.
  • Better for lines that clog every few months or years.

If a cable already stalled partway down your line, do not keep forcing it. That stall is information, and a camera should read it before anyone sends water in.

A backup that will not clear: what to do right now

These situations call for a plumber the same day, and some for tonight.

Sewage in the lowest fixtures

Wastewater in a shower, tub or floor drain means the line is blocked downstream. Keep every tap off and call.

A cable that stopped short

If someone ran a snake and it jammed or came back with nothing, stop. The blockage may be a broken section, which jetting would not cure.

Several units on one line

In a duplex, rental or small commercial building, tell the manager and have everyone stop using water. One blocked line takes down every unit it serves.

Slow drains and a faint sewer smell

This is not a midnight call. A same-day appointment when available gives time for a camera look before jetting.

After hours, the call reaches dispatch and the plumber helps decide whether this needs a visit tonight or first thing in the morning.

A jetting visit, camera first

Jetting follows a look inside the pipe, not the other way round.

Camera through the line

A technician can run a small camera from a cleanout or removed fixture to see grease, roots, scale, sags or breaks. This is where a repair-not-clean call gets made.

Decide and price

If the pipe is intact and coated with debris, jetting is proposed. You get a clear price before work starts, and a damaged pipe gets a repair conversation instead.

Jet from the access point

The hose and nozzle go in through the cleanout and the line is washed section by section toward the main.

Camera again, then flow test

A second pass shows the walls now. Water is run at the fixtures to confirm the line carries a normal load.

Where jetting fits and where it does not

The pipe material and condition decide this, which is why the camera comes first.

Pipe or problemJetting a good fit?Why
Grease buildupUsually yesWater scours the film that a cable only drills through.
Roots at a jointOften, with a catchRoots can be cut back, but the open joint will let them return.

Jetting is not priced the same on every line. These are the factors.

Restaurants and shopping centers on SR 50 and Fifty West

The city's Fifty West district runs along SR 50 between SR 429 and Clarke Road, so busy food and retail lines are part of the local picture.

Food businesses and interceptors

The city's sewer-use manual, an undated draft on its site, says nonresidential food businesses shall have an approved oil and grease interceptor. Jetting the line past the interceptor does not replace servicing the interceptor, so ask who maintains it. Confirm current rules with Ocoee Utilities.

Shared lines in strip centers

In a building with several tenants, one kitchen can clog a line others share. A camera pass traces which tenant's line the grease is coming from.

Apartments and multifamily

The same manual lets the utility order a multifamily property found to cause stoppages to stop sending grease down the line or to install an interceptor. A repeat stoppage there is a reason to fix the cause, not just rinse it out.

For a main line that serves more than one building or unit, see sewer line cleaning. For a single fixture, drain cleaning is usually enough, and we cover neighbors such as Apopka too.
