A bedroom community from 1953: what the housing era means for pipes

If sewage or water is coming into a Pine Hills home right now, call our 24/7 emergency line. Stop running water, keep the family out of the affected room, and leave the rest to dispatch.

Orange County's Pine Hills plan says the community began in 1953 with the Robinswood and Pine Ridge Estates subdivisions. It was built as a bedroom community for Martin Marietta workers and was prominent in the 1960s and 1970s. County records show earlier plats too, Pine Hills Sub No. 1 in 1949 and Pine Hills Manor in 1951.

Homes from that stretch have had decades to wear. We found no source dating the pipe materials, so we do not guess. Instead, a camera look at the drain line is the honest way to learn the condition. See our sewer inspection page, and consider repiping if supply lines keep leaking.

Septic to sewer

Septic tanks, sewer connections and the retrofit on Pine Hills Rd

The county plan says sewer service reached only limited parts of Pine Hills at first, so septic tanks are still present. The Pine Hills Septic Tank Retrofit Project moved parcels along Pine Hills Rd, between Alhambra Dr and Golf Club Pkwy, onto sewer. The county says all benefitting properties connected, and its 2025 report notes that plumbing permits rose with the project.

What that means for you is practical. Know whether your home sits on a septic tank or the Orange County Utilities sewer, because a backup means a different repair in each case. A broken line from the house to the sewer connection may need sewer line repair, while a clog in the house branch can be handled with drain cleaning.

Before we arrive: Pine Hills emergencies and safe first steps

Call the 24/7 line first for these. Our emergency dispatch decides with you whether a plumber is needed tonight.

Sewage in the lowest tub or floor drain

Stop every fixture and the washer. On a septic lot, do not keep flushing to see if it clears.

A burst or spraying supply line

Find the shutoff where the water line enters the house and turn it clockwise until the flow stops. Open a faucet to drain the lines.

Water coming through the ceiling

Move furniture, put a bucket under the drip, and stay out of rooms with wet light fixtures. A burst pipe needs fast attention.

Gas odor

Leave the house first. Call 911 or the gas utility from outside, then call us for plumbing work once it is safe.

A single slow drain can often be booked for a same-day appointment when available. You do not need an emergency visit for that.

Permits

County permits for Pine Hills plumbing work

The Orange County Division of Building Safety covers plumbing in Pine Hills. Applications go through its Fast Track portal, and help is available at 407-836-8160. A repipe or a sewer connection often needs a permit, so ask before work starts.

The county's page also says that under Florida's 2026 law HB 803, from July 1, 2026 some single-family residential plumbing jobs under $7,500 must be exempt from permits, with other conditions. Whether your job qualifies is the county's call, so confirm with the Division first.
