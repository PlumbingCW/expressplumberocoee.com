What a clogged drain really is, and what to do right now

If dirty water is climbing in a sink, tub or floor drain in Ocoee, call our 24/7 line now. While you wait, stop running water into that drain, keep the washer and dishwasher off, and keep kids and pets out of any standing water.

Drain cleaning is the removal of whatever has narrowed or blocked a drain pipe so wastewater flows downhill again. In a home that is usually grease, hair, soap scum, paper or wipes lining a pipe only a couple of inches wide.

A clog is rarely one solid plug. Debris builds on the pipe wall, catches more debris, and shrinks the opening a little with each use. That is why most drains slow down for weeks before they stop. Drain and vent layout in Florida follows the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code, so clearing a line should never involve changing how it is vented.

How a clog announces itself

The symptom tells you roughly where the blockage sits, which decides how a plumber attacks it.

Gurgling or bubbling

Air is being pulled past a partial blockage. A toilet that gurgles when the shower drains usually shares a pipe with it.

Slow, then slower

The pipe is narrowing but not shut. A sink that takes minutes to empty is a clog still in progress.

Backs up in a different fixture

Washer water in the tub or kitchen water in the laundry sink means both drains meet downstream of the clog.

Clears, then returns

A plunger opened a hole but the buildup on the pipe wall is still there. Expect the same drain to slow again.

When a clogged drain is an emergency

Most clogs are a nuisance. These are the ones that should not wait for the morning.

Dirty water in the lowest fixture

Sewage or gray water rising in a tub, shower or floor drain is coming back from further down the line. Call now.

Two or more fixtures backing up

When the toilet, tub and a sink all misbehave together, the blockage is probably past where the branches join. See main line cleaning.

Water reaching walls, floors or outlets

Shut the supply valve under the fixture if you can reach it dry, then call. Never stand in water near a powered outlet.

One fixture slow, everything else normal

This can usually be booked as a same-day appointment when available. You do not need a midnight visit for a sluggish bathroom sink.

After hours, the call goes to dispatch and the plumber sorts the urgent jobs from the ones that can wait for daylight.

Skip the bottle of chemical opener while you wait. The EPA advises avoiding chemical drain openers, and caustic liquid left in the pipe is a hazard once a cable or water starts moving through it.

Cable, jetting or chemical cleaner: an honest comparison

No single tool wins every clog. The choice depends on what is in the pipe and how often it returns.

Cable machine (snaking)

  • A turning steel cable with a cutting head breaks up or hooks the blockage.
  • Fits a single sink, tub or toilet branch with one local clog.
  • Opens a channel through grease rather than scraping the wall, so a grease clog can close again.
  • Reaches a long way but cannot see what it is hitting.

Water jetting

  • A nozzle on a hose scours the whole inside of the pipe with water.
  • Suits lines that clog again and again, or ones coated with grease and scale.
  • Does not repair a cracked, offset or collapsed pipe.
  • Should follow a camera look when the pipe is old or the history is unclear.

Chemical cleaners sit in a third category. They work only where they can reach the blockage, and in a standing-water drain they often cannot. If the same line keeps closing, ask about hydro jetting after the immediate clog is cleared.

What the visit looks like

You should know what is happening in your house at every point.

Trace the clog

The plumber asks which fixtures are affected, since when, and what has been tried. Running water at other fixtures shows which ones share the blocked pipe.

Clear price, then work

Once the clog is located and the tool is chosen, you get a clear price before any work starts. Nothing begins until you agree.

Clear the line

The plumber works through the trap, a cleanout or the nearest access, using cable or another method suited to the pipe.

Prove it is clear

Water runs at volume for several minutes at the affected fixture and at its neighbors. If the clog came back fast or looks structural, you hear that before the plumber leaves.

What Ocoee drains are up against

Local rules and local infrastructure shape which clogs show up and who handles what.

The city's grease letter

The City of Ocoee's New Resident Grease Control Letter says not to put greasy foods down the sink or into the garbage disposal, and to let grease cool in a jar and bin it. A disposal grinds food but does nothing about grease, so scrape plates dry if your kitchen line keeps clogging.

The FOG page

The city's Fats, Oils and Grease page warns that grease can clog pipes and cause sewer backups in the community. It recommends a sink strainer and wiping pans before washing, the cheapest clog prevention in the house.

City sewer, with some septic

Ocoee's own utility runs wastewater collection for close to 30,000 residents. An older city planning document says some septic tanks were still in use when it was written. Tell the plumber which system you have, because the EPA warns that drain chemicals can harm septic systems.

Orange County mains

Orange County Utilities tells its own customers to call a certified plumber first for a sewage backup, and says it does not handle private sewer lines. Ocoee runs a separate utility, so check which one bills you, and keep the plumber's receipt either way.

What drives the price of drain cleaning

Two clogged drains can cost very different amounts. These are the reasons.

Where the clog sitsA trap under a sink is a short job. A branch line behind a wall or the main line takes more time and equipment.
Access to the pipeA cleanout near the house is quick. With no cleanout, the plumber may have to pull a toilet or work from a roof vent.
Method neededA cable run costs differently from jetting or a camera inspection. The right tool is whichever clears the cause.
Time of the callNight, weekend and same-day work involves different scheduling than a planned daytime visit.
What the clog turns out to beGrease and hair are one job. Roots, a broken pipe or a belly in the line may mean a separate repair quote.

When it is bigger than one drain

Cleanouts, roots and the main line

A cleanout is a capped pipe that gives direct access to the sewer line, often outdoors near the house. With one, a plumber can run cable straight in without pulling a toilet. Not every home has one that is easy to find.

The city's sewer-use manual defines the lateral as the service line that runs from the public sewer to a point five feet outside the building wall. It does not say plainly who repairs which stretch, so ask Ocoee Utilities before assuming a repair is the city's or yours.

Roots enter where moisture and nutrients leak out, usually at a joint. UF/IFAS notes roots grow wherever conditions are good and can extend three to four times the drip line. A clog that returns at the same spot, with fine roots on the cable, calls for a sewer camera inspection.
