Main line clogged: every drain in the house is backing up right now

Sewer line cleaning in Ocoee is the job of clearing the one large pipe that all your drains share. When it blocks, wastewater has nowhere to go but up, so call our 24/7 line and, while you wait, stop using water entirely. Do not flush, run the washer or take a shower.

Sewage in the lowest tub or floor drain

The lowest opening in the house gives way first. Keep people and pets away from it and do not try to mop it up with the water still running.

Toilets bubble when other fixtures drain

Air and water are both being forced back up the line. This is usually the last warning before an overflow.

Water standing at the outdoor cleanout

If the cleanout cap is wet or water is pooling at it, leave the cap on. Opening it can release the full backup into the yard.

A rental or multi-unit building

One main line may serve several units. Tell the property manager so every household stops using water at once.

Same-day appointments are offered when available, and after-hours calls reach dispatch so the plumber can say whether this needs a visit tonight.

Main line or just one fixture? A two-minute check

The answer decides whether this is a quick drain job or a full line clearing.

Lowest fixture fails first

A shower or floor drain that backs up before the sinks upstairs points to the main line, because gravity fills the lowest opening first.

A flush changes another drain

If flushing a toilet gurgles a tub or bubbles a sink, those drains share the blocked pipe.

Only one fixture is slow

A single sluggish sink is a branch clog. Hydro jetting or cabling a branch is a different job from the main.

Soft or sinking ground over the line

The Florida Geological Survey lists broken sewer and drain pipes among the causes of ground sinking that mimics a sinkhole. Do not pour water into a hole, and get the line looked at.

Cable, jetting or a bottle of cleaner for a main line

A main line is long and often wider than a household branch, which changes the trade-offs.

Cable on a long run

  • Reaches far down a line from a cleanout.
  • Cuts through root masses and bulky obstructions.
  • Leaves a coating and root stubs behind.
  • Cannot tell you what the pipe looks like.

Water jetting

  • Washes grease and silt from the walls over the whole run.
  • Fits a main that clogs again and again.
  • Is not a repair for a broken or offset pipe.
  • Works best after a camera has checked the pipe.

Cleaner from a bottle is the weakest option here. The EPA advises avoiding chemical drain openers, and in a main already full of water the product dilutes before it reaches the blockage.

The Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code, calls for accessible cleanouts on drain lines, so ask the plumber where yours is before the visit.

How a main line visit goes

The plumber works from the pipe outward, not from a guess.

Find the way in

The plumber looks for the cleanout, usually outdoors near the house. With none, access comes through a removed toilet or a roof vent.

Price, then clear

You get a clear price before work starts. Then the line is cleared with the tool that fits what is blocking it.

Read what came out

Grease, roots, wipes and mud look different on the cable or in the water. Soil or pipe fragments suggest a break, which means sewer line repair may be next.

Test the whole house

Water runs at several fixtures and the cleanout is checked. A line that clears and then refills fast gets a camera recommendation.

Grease, roots and wipes in Ocoee main lines

Three habits and one storm pattern account for many main line problems here.

Grease

The City of Ocoee's FOG page says grease can cause clogs and sewer backups. A kitchen that sends grease down the drain feeds the main line, so scrape and bin it.

Roots

UF/IFAS says roots grow wherever moisture and nutrients exist and can reach three to four times the drip line. A root clog means a joint is leaking, so cutting roots without a camera look is only a pause.

Wipes

Ocoee's pages we read say nothing about wipes, but UF/IFAS says flushable wipes break down more slowly than toilet paper and may cause major sewer blockages. Treat the bin as the only safe place for them.

Storm backups

Orange County Utilities says that when power is out, pump stations may be down and sewer backups can result. That is county guidance, and Ocoee runs its own utility, so ask neighbors whether they are backing up too before blaming your pipe.
