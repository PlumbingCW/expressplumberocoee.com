Do this first: stop the water, power or gas

Water heater repair in Ocoee starts with a safe shutoff, and our 24/7 line is open if you cannot make one. While you wait, close the cold-water valve on the pipe above the tank, switch off its breaker if the panel is dry, and keep everyone out of standing water.

For a gas heater, one rule outranks the rest. If you smell gas, leave, touch no switches, and call the gas utility from outside. If Peoples Gas serves you, its line is 1-800-400-4271. Do not relight a pilot in a room that smells of gas.

Never cap or plug the discharge pipe from the relief valve, and never drain a hot tank onto the floor. A blocked relief valve removes the tank's only pressure exit, and hot water scalds.

Signs your water heater needs repair, and what usually causes each

The symptom narrows the cause fast. A technician still confirms it with a meter or a gauge before replacing anything.

No hot water at all

On electric units, think tripped breaker, a high-limit reset or a failed element. On gas units, think pilot out, a worn thermocouple or a closed gas valve.

Lukewarm or runs out fast

A dead lower element, a cracked dip tube that mixes cold water at the top, or sediment smothering the burner can each shrink your hot water supply.

Popping or rumbling

Minerals settle on the tank floor and trap steam bubbles that pop as they escape. Flushing helps a younger tank. On an old one, heavy rumbling is a warning.

Water around the base

The source may be a fitting, the drain valve, the relief valve, condensation or the tank shell. A shell leak ends repair. A wet floor that returns after a dry heater can also mean a line leak, which leak detection sorts out.

Rusty or brown hot water

Run only the cold tap. If cold is clear and hot is brown, the tank interior or anode rod is corroding. If both run brown, the pipes are the cause.

Sulfur smell on the hot side only

This often points to bacteria reacting with the anode rod inside the tank. A flush or an anode swap may fix it. A smell on cold water too comes from the supply.

No hot water or a wet base? Call dispatch.

Gas, electric and heat pump heaters fail in different places

The fuel decides what the technician tests and how long a repair takes. The same symptom, no hot water, leads to three different checklists. Under the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, which is based on the International Plumbing Code, a tank heater needs a relief valve with a discharge pipe, so a repair that leaves that line missing or capped is not finished.

Gas heaters

The pilot or igniter, the thermocouple, the burner and the gas control valve make up most gas repairs. The technician also checks the flue draft, because soot or scorch marks near the top mean exhaust is spilling into the room.

Replacing a gas control valve is licensed work, not a weekend fix. A bad joint leaks gas, and the part needs a leak test afterward.

Electric heaters

Two elements and two thermostats sit behind small panels, with a high-limit reset above the upper thermostat. A meter shows which part has lost continuity. Elements must stay submerged. Power returned to a drained tank destroys them in moments.

Hybrid heat pump heaters

These add a compressor, a fan, an air filter and a condensate drain to the tank. Common faults are a clogged filter, a plugged condensate line or a unit stuck in electric-only mode that raises your bill. Sealed-system repairs are brand-specific and may sit under the manufacturer's warranty, so check your paperwork first.

Tell dispatch your brand and model when you call. We do not diagnose heat pump faults over the phone.

Tank leaking or flooding at night

At 2 a.m. the choice is simple. Stop the water, protect the floor, and get a plumber booked. After-hours repair calls follow the same safe-shutoff steps as daytime ones.

Water pouring from the tank

Close the cold valve on top. If the valve will not turn, close the house main. Then switch off the heater's power or gas.

Hissing or steam from the relief pipe

Stay clear of the pipe, since the water can scald. Switch off power or gas at the heater. This can mean high pressure or overheating, and it needs a technician.

Electric heater and wet floor

Do not step into water to reach a breaker. If the panel is dry and reachable, turn the heater's breaker off. Otherwise stay out and tell dispatch.

Burning or gas smell

Leave the house. Call the gas utility or 911 from outside. A plumber visit follows only once the area is safe.

The City of Ocoee is responsible for the water line up to and including the meter. A leak ahead of the meter is the city's problem, and its Utilities after-hours line is 407-428-5766. Everything from the meter to the faucets is the owner's, including the heater.

If the tank shell has failed, repair is not possible, and water heater replacement is the next step.

What decides the cost of a water heater repair

A thermocouple and a shell failure are very different bills. These are the factors behind the clear price you get before work starts.

The failed partElements, thermostats and thermocouples cost far less than a gas control valve or a heat pump component.
Brand and model availabilityA common tank has parts on hand. A less common brand or a heat pump model may need a part ordered, which can turn a same-day repair into a later visit.
Access and locationA heater in an attic or tight closet takes longer to reach and test than one in a garage.
Diagnosis timeIntermittent faults take longer to chase. The technician has to catch the fault while it is happening.
Tank age against repair costOn an old tank, a repair that costs a large part of a new unit usually favors replacing instead, or moving to a tankless water heater if the home suits it. We say so plainly.

Homeowners insurance typically covers sudden and accidental water damage, not wear, slow leaks over time, or the heater itself. Ask your insurer before repairs begin, and keep photographs of the leak and the data label.

Hard water scale: what 5 to 6 grains per gallon means for your heater

Ocoee Utilities' 2025 water quality report lists hardness at 5 to 6 grains per gallon. The city's own scale calls 5 to 12 moderate. Forest Lake Estates gets Orange County Utilities water, and the table is not labeled by area, so check your own supplier's report.

Moderate hardness leaves scale on elements and sediment on the tank floor over time. We do not say it shortens heater life in Ocoee, because no source shows that. The practical step is simple. Run a test strip on your tap, and ask the technician to show you what comes out of the drain valve.
