What this site collects

This website has no forms, accounts or comment boxes, so it does not ask for personal information. We do not set cookies of our own. The page loads fonts from Google Fonts and may load a Google Maps embed, and those services receive your IP address and browser details under Google's own policies.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded or monitored for quality and training purposes. When you call, we collect your phone number, the address of the job and what you tell us about the problem, so a plumber can be sent and a price can be given.

We use that information to dispatch and complete the work, to follow up on it and to meet legal and insurance requirements. We do not sell it.

Sharing

Job details are shared with the plumber assigned to your call and with the permitting office when a permit is required. We may share information when the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold about your call, or ask us to delete it where the law allows, by emailing contact@expressplumberocoee.com or calling (321) 463-9969. If this policy changes, the date above changes with it.
